# Scary Halloween

We dressed up as Skrimmel Skrammel for halloween. Quite good group costume, if I should say so myself, but sadly **no one!** who understood what we were. So strange, haven't they heard about *Cheezy Keys* down here??

![Skrimmel Skrammel](./images/2026-10-31_Kostyme.jpg)

And it was really that scary, I'll admit it. What was actually quite scary, was that Sanna (dressed as Hans Petter), decided to make a TikTok (see it [here](https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZS9yqhFbxx34r-Vw7dG/)), which now have more than 74 000 views and around 10 000 likes. How am I supposed to show my face in Norway now, I'm going to be stopped in the street all the time. How annoying.

Og det var ikke egentlig så skummelt, det kan jeg innrømme. Det som derimot var ganske skummelt var at Sanna (hun som er kledd ut som Hans Petter), bestemte seg for å lage en TikTok (se den [her](https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZS9yqhFbxx34r-Vw7dG/)), som nå har fått 74 000 views og rundt 10 000 likes. Hvordan skal jeg liksom vise ansiktet mitt i Norge igjen nå, kommer til å bli stoppet på åpen gate hele tiden. Så utrolig mas.
