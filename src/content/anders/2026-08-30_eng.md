# Verified recommendations

I apaologize to everyone who read the forst post and listened to *Chapter One* by Lematire and didn't think it was that good. I listened to it today and didn't think it was that good either. Hence, I have made a list with some albums of electronic music I can actually say I like.

![Album](./images/2026-08-30_Album.png)

1. ***Settle* by Disclosure:** Very good beach techno. Some also call it deep house. I recommend avoiding *Special Edition*, as it is three hours long. Choose instead *Deluxe Version*, which is only 80 minutes.
2. ***Flume* by Flume:** Imagine the new he has made, but instead of much being good and some being weird, the most is good.
3. ***Fibonacci* by Subtronics:** Music that says 🐭🚨🐭🚨🐭🚨. I'll see this guy in Brisbane December 12, if anyone wants to join
4. ***The Beginning* by Broiler:** Dj Broilers first songs. Also Dj Broilers best songs. For some reason in opposite chronologial order.
