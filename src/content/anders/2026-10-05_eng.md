# Proof I have friends

Last time I spoke to my mum she asked me if I have gotten any friends yet. And I answered yes, mom, I have. I felt that the other side of the phone was not entirely convinced, so here is the proof.

This weekend I went to Raglan to surf. I have been to Raglan before, but then the waves were too cool for us. Luckily the waves were kinder to beginners this time. I drove with Sanna and Juvan. Sanna is from Sola (not Stavanger) and Juvan is from Narvik (definitely not Harstad).

![Surfe](./images/2026-10-05_Surfe.jpg)

Last time I tried to surf I said it was fun, but that I'd prefer standing up on the board over just falling off all the time. This time I managed to actually stand a bit more, and that was way more fun. So there's a little surf tip from me to you.

We also taste tested a windscreen wash colored soda. It tasted just as natural as it looks. May be good mixed with [Små Sure Sour Shot Bubble Fizz](https://www.vinmonopolet.no/Land/Danmark/Sm%C3%A5-Sure-Sour-Shot-Bubble-Fizz/p/7271102).
