# Don't want to work with you either

I have a course where every assignment is done in pairs. Since I don't known anybody else that takes this course, I was assigned a random dude to do the assignment with. The course is called Multivariable Control Systems, thanks for asking. Working with thus dude went quite well, so we agreed on working together on the next assignment too. The dude doesn't sign up to the same group as me, or any group for that matter, and I get a new random assignee. So I write to him on Canvas:

*"Hi! I already agreed to work with another guy in our class, but we just failed to sign up to the same group. Is it okay for you to leave this group and join his after? His group number is 44. I am sorry for this, but I hope you understand"*

This is of course not the best formulation in the world, but it isn't that bad either. He answers, obviously, with empathically and proportionally.

*"Sure, I can leave. I think it's pretty ridiculous that you apparently already had someone to work with but waited until after the groups were assigned to realise you should have joined their group. I ended up in this group because I didn't have anyone else to work with, so it's frustrating to be left dealing with the consequences of your mistake. It reflects poorly on your personal character, so it's good I don't have to work with you."*

Well, maybe I think your lack of knowledge about international humanitarian law "reflects poorly on your personal character". So it's good I don't have to work with you. Owned.
