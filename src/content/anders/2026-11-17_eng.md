# Happy half year day

Since everything is upside down in New Zealand, November is May and May is November. That means 17th of November in New Zealand is actually 17th of May in Norway. Then there is only one thing to say:

NORWAY!!! Happy half year day!! 🔴⚪🔵⚪🔴

We naturally celebrated with the traditional champagne breakfast

![Bord](./images/2026-11-17_Spise.jpg)

I'll call it a success. And then we went for karaoke in the evening:)
