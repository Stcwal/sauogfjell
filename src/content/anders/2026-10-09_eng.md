# 4 days with Auckland Scuba

And just like that, my time at [Auckland Scuba](https://www.aucklandscubadive.co.nz/) is over 🌊

4 days.

12 meters.

No room for error.

This week I have been lucky enough to attend a course with [Auckland Scuba](https://www.aucklandscubadive.co.nz/) to be certified as a for å bli sertifisert som PADI® Open Water Diver. 🤿🐟

Over the past four days I have learned more than I could imagine, and am left with valuable experience in how to survive and thrive in places I didn't know it was possible.

This course has really taught me how important it is to remain calm in stressful situations in extreme environments, experience I know I will carry with me for the rest of my life.

I am now both deeply humble and incredibly proud to call myself a certified PADI® Open Water Diver. 🌟

A big thanks to everyone at [Auckland Scuba](https://www.aucklandscubadive.co.nz/) for making this week so great, and for giving me this fantastic opportunity! 🙌🏼🦈
