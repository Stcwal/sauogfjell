# Trip to Turangi

This weekend I went to Turangi.

On friday, I tried fly fishing for the first time. Turangi is the fly fishing capital of New Zealand, and I am really glad i didn't come there with a spin rod. That would have been embarassing. I actually didn't come with any rod, but could rent at the local fish store. 

On saturday, I tried fly fishing for the second time.

![Fiske](./images/2026-10-19_Fiske.jpeg)

Did I get any fish? No. Did I enjoy myself? No. Was the weather nice? No. Was it nice being outside? No.

On sunday, I walked what is supposed to be New Zealand's best day trip. It is called Tongariro Crossing, and was absolutely a great day trip. Yet again, the prove to my skeptical mother that I have friends, here is the hiking party in front of Mount Doom.

![Turfølge](./images/2026-10-19_Turfølge.jpeg)

At on point, I took the picture below. I think the view was nice at least. This day was much better than the really bad days friday and saturday, where I didn't have any fun at any point.
