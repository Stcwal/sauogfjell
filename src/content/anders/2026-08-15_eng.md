# Tried surfing today

This is my first travel letter, since it is the first time I went on a travel letter-worthy travel(?), despite it only being a day trip. Previous travels(?) have included a busride to a sports store and a walk to the grocery store, both of which I consider boring.

Today I travelled to Te Arai with a bunch of other students, all of them part of the University of Auckland Surf Society, to surf!. THe day started at 0800 at the rental car dealer where we picked up our beautiful Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid. It had the steering wheel on the wrong side, and was apperently also supposed to be driven on the wrong side of the road. That was a great trial, that luckily went well. 

![Bil](./images/2026-08-15_Bil.jpg)

Arriving at the beach. Car, driver and passengers in good shape. The beach was very long and beautiful! (See picture). We also got lucky with the weather! Comfy 15 degrees and spotwise sun. And during the time of the year where it is supposedly most winter. 

![Strand](./images/2026-08-15_Strand.jpg)

All that remained was to surf. That is apparently not that easy, so today was definetely the day where I *tried* surfing for the first time. Towards the end of the day, I managed to catch a wave, to my great joy. And then I fell off. I was both tired and happy. I tried some other rounds, but the tank was empty. Rating: 10/10. Will do it again, and then I will do it properly!
