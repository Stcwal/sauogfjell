# Mid-semester break post 4

Here are the last days of the mid-semester break. Because of low motivation, this will be short and with few pictures. Sue me.

## Friday 2026-09-11

First tourist "attraction" of the day, was a boiling puddle of acidic mud. This place is called "Waiotapu Mud Pool", and it is actually quite cool that nature has produced it. But it didn't really knock the proverbial socks off

![Gjørme](./images/2026-09-13_Gjørme.jpg)

Afterwards we walked to a *very* big waterfall. Its name is Tarawera. The road to the parking lot for the trail to this waterfall goes through a private forest, but the land owners are *so* kind and allow access through the forest on weekends, but only if you meet at the reception and pay 10 kiwidollars. Sadly, we did not see the sign that said this before we were driving away again. Oops. I doubt the New Zealand police surveill this blog, so it's fine admitting this serious offence. The moral of the story is that it is okay to steal as long as you don't get caught. The waterfall was cool though, so it was worth it. 

![Tarawera](./images/2026-09-13_Tarawera.jpg)

We swam in the river in the way down. This picture does in no way capture how beautifil this place was. I choose to blame the photographer and not the motif
