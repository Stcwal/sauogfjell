# Mid-semester break post 2

Here is the next post from the mid-semester break. I am hopefully a bit better at spelling this time.

This vacation has turned out to be a real German experience, something I have frequently felt the last few days. 

## Friday 2026-09-04

The day started with a small hike to gaze upon the local oil refinery. That made the Germans really happy. Even though they don't really have that many oil refineries in Germany (no footnote on that claim), and this then wasn't *quite* like home, it was still nice to alleviate the industry withdrawal a little bit.

![Oljeraffineri](./images/2026-09-06_Oljeraffineri.jpg)

The rest of the day mostly traveling. But we made a mandatory pit stop at a German bakery. Haha we don't you try ordering in German, Anders?? That would be really funny. Talk about jokes at someone elses expense. I (we) bought bread and brezel. In some way

![Brezel](./images/2026-09-06_Brezel.jpg)

We ended the day at a camp site, yet another safe port for the German. I kinda understand why they like it, because it was actually quite alright. Maybe because it was offseasen and very few people there. But that is just a hypothesis.

![Campingplass](./images/2026-09-06_Campingplass.jpg)

## Saturday 2026-09-05

Today we initially wanted to surf, since we had travelled to a sort of surf town, that was a suiting activity. But the surf dude at the surf shop said the surf waves maybe possibly were a tiny bit too gnarly for beginners. Sadly no wapow rip curl brother today. We tried renting kayaks as an alternatie activity, but the kayak rental place was closed for the winter. No watersport today. Instead it became a lazy day in the sun, then hike to a big waterfall. Cool waterfall. It was big. 

![Foss](./images/2026-09-06_Foss.jpg)

We also looked at some windmills. The Germans are known for loving their wind mills. No one wanted to fight though.

![Vindmøller](./images/2026-09-06_Vindmøller.jpg)

Without us knowing, there was a ukulele festival in Ragland today. Luckily we heard that there would be a concert in the evening. Here is a picture from that concert:

![Konsert](./images/2026-09-06_Konsert.jpg)

Notice that everyone on stage is a bit old. It was the same case for the audience, we definetely pulled the average down, and that with quite a lot. Still, we had nothing to say about the skills on stage or the energy of the crowd. I think I've never seen that cool granddads, and I am already looking forward to bingo club in 50 years.

In addition to the granddads, there were also a ukulele group with 13 aunts, and a guy with the world's only baritone-ukulele. So if anyone wants to come with me to Raglan Blues & Country Festival 23-26 October and see the gang again, let me know. I am going.
