# Passed the climbing test

New Zealand loves rules. But not anybody elses. So to be allowed to climb and belay lead here it is not good enough with the Norwegian steep climbing certificate. Instead one of the employees at the climbing centre has to watch you climb and belay lead, to approve that you can do it at that specific climbing gym. If you want to climb in another gym you will please and thankyou have to do the test there as well. 

I tried taking the test last week, but the 16 year old examinator was like "You don't know how to use the grigri". 
And I was like "I know, it's the first time I've used one". And he was like "I cannot pass you, you don't know how to use it". I was like "Isn't the point to keep the climber from dying. This was always safer than the ATC". And he was like "You don't know how to use it. You must practice more". And I was like "How am I supposed to practice if I am not allowed to belay". And I was like "You can try again next time". And I was like not happy with failing the belay test.

But I passed this week! Then you get an exclusive, pink card, which you can carry around if you want to. So now I am a Certified Lead Climber at Birkenhead Pool and Leisure Center. LinkedIn post coming soon.
